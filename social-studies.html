Review key concepts, test your understanding and prepare for assessment. These are practice materials, not official KJSEA examination questions.

1. Multipurpose River Projects

Meaning: Projects that use river water to serve several purposes.

Uses include:

  • Generating hydroelectric power.
  • Irrigating crops.
  • Controlling floods.
  • Providing water for homes and industries.
  • Supporting fishing, recreation and tourism.

Possible challenges: Displacement of communities, high construction costs, changes to river ecosystems and the loss of farmland.

2. Map Skills

  • Scale: Shows the relationship between distance on a map and distance on the ground.
  • Direction: Describes the position of one place in relation to another.
  • Grid references: Help locate places on a map.
  • Contours: Lines joining places of equal height above sea level.

Example: If a map scale is 1:50,000, then 1 cm on the map represents 50,000 cm, or 500 metres, on the ground.

3. Practice Questions

Question 1: State two benefits of multipurpose river projects.

Examples: hydroelectric power generation and irrigation. Other valid benefits include flood control and water supply.

Question 2: A map has a scale of 1:50,000. Two places are 4 cm apart on the map. Calculate their ground distance in kilometres.

4 × 50,000 = 200,000 cm = 2 km.

Question 3: Explain two challenges associated with constructing dams.

Examples: communities may be displaced, construction is expensive, and natural habitats may be affected.
